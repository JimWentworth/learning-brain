The resultsHigher, and fairer

Mastery programs raised exam scores by about half a standard deviation on average. The benefit was larger for weaker students, so mastery learning narrowed the gap, rather than widening it. Mastery programs also had moderate, positive effects on how students felt about the subject and the teaching.

The costTime, and pace

Self-paced mastery programs sometimes lowered course completion, as students fell behind. Mastery programs can increase the time students spend on instruction. And retakes take time for instructors too. So design matters: retakes work best when they require new learning between attempts, use new versions of questions, and fit a clear schedule.

For teaching

Choose the few outcomes that truly matter, and allow a retake on those. Require a short correction or study step before a retake. Use fresh questions, and set deadlines so students keep pace. Share this tip with students: use a retake to fix what you missed.
