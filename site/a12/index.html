The designBefore, during, and after AI

Before AI: commitStudents predict, draft and generate ideas on their own, so there is something to learn from, and something to compare against.
During AI: thinkThe tool hints rather than solves, asks before it tells, and runs on correct material. Students explain its reasoning back, and look for a flaw.
After AI: checkStudents verify sources, check their confidence against results, and do some practice without AI, so everyone can see what they can do alone.
