Thinking alongside AI From making to checking

Knowledge workers who trusted AI more reported less critical thinking; those more confident in their own ability reported more. The thinking did not vanish. It moved, from making to checking.

Three hundred and nineteen knowledge workers described 936 real examples of using generative AI at work, and how much critical thinking each one took. All of them used AI at work at least once a week. Hao-Ping Lee and colleagues at Carnegie Mellon University and Microsoft Research reported the results in 2025.

Two kinds of confidence pulled in opposite directions. The more people trusted the AI on a task, the less critical thinking they reported. The more confident they were in their own ability, the more critical thinking they reported.

Less effortFreed up, or switched off

In most examples, workers felt that AI reduced the effort of critical thinking. That can free effort for higher-level work. Or it can mean the thinking simply stops.

A shiftCritical thinking moved

Critical thinking did not vanish. It shifted in three ways:

VerifyingFrom gathering information to verifying it.
IntegratingFrom solving problems to integrating AI responses.
StewardingFrom doing tasks to stewarding them: deciding what the AI should do, and judging whether it did it.

BarriersWhy people didn’t check

People also named the barriers: lack of time; a sense that thinking critically was not part of the job; and difficulty judging AI output in areas where they lacked expertise. Some even said AI added effort, because its answers had to be verified.

A cautionSelf-reports, not causes

This is a survey of self-reports. It shows associations, not causes, and people may not judge their own thinking accurately.

Make checking visible

Make verification visible and graded: ask students to show what they checked, and how. Build their confidence in the subject itself, since self-confidence went with more critical thinking. And teach stewardship explicitly: deciding what the AI should do, and judging whether it did it.
