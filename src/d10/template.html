WhyNot the technology

The authors warned against crediting the medium. The blended conditions often included extra learning time, extra resources, and more chances to practise. The advantage reflected redesigning the learning experience, not the technology itself.

A cautionOlder studies, older learners

The studies ran through 2008 and were mostly with older learners, with very few in schools. And they studied planned online courses, not the emergency remote teaching of 2020, which was a different thing.

The lesson echoes the Belonging series: format matters less than structure. A well-designed online course beats a poorly designed lecture, and a well-designed blend can beat both.

For teaching

When you move material online, redesign it rather than recording it. Use online time for what it does well, like self-paced practice with feedback. Use in-person time for discussion, problem solving and questions. Share this tip with students: treat online work as real class time.
