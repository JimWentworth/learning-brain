The designBefore, during, and after

BeforeCheck what the room and materials say. Plan structure into class time. Share that early struggle is common and temporary.
DuringActive learning with clear structure. Wise feedback. A range of people students can see themselves in. Ability as something that grows, in words and policies.
AfterLook at outcomes by group, not just averages. Read feedback for signs of doubt. Spell out the unwritten rules. Tell students what you changed.

Before high-stakes moments, like a first exam, you might consider a brief values affirmation, knowing that effects are small and vary by setting. And present tests as a chance to show growth, not a verdict on a group.
